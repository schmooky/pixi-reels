Add: `attachReelSounds(reelSet, port, cues)` snaps a table of sound cues onto a reel set's events: a `spin` loop from `spin:start` to `spin:allLanded`, a `reelStop` one-shot per landing reel, an `anticipation` loop per teasing reel with a pitch that can climb by `order`, `skip`, `win`, `cascadeDestroy`, and `on` for any other event. `attachBoardSounds(board, port, cues)` is the same table over a Hold & Win board (`featureEnter`, `respinStart`, `cellLanded` with the cell and whether it hit, `coinLocked`, `boardFull`, `featureEnd`, `on`). A cue can wait `delay` seconds after its beat (a thud timed to the bottom of the landing bounce). Every cue sees the active `speed`, its `profile`, and the spin's `mode` (`reelSet.spinMode`, new: `'standard'` or `'cascade'`) and can stay quiet with `when(ctx)`. Loops fade in and out; `destroy()` detaches and stops everything. `zvukPort(engine)` adapts a `@schmooky/zvuk` engine (sounds or variants by name, a missing name warns once) with no dependency on zvuk.
