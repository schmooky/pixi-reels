## MultiWays

Per-spin row variation: each reel lands on a different row count between minCells and maxCells, and total ways is the product of per-reel rows.

**Gotcha:** call reelSet.setShape(rowsPerReel) BEFORE setResult(), with serverGrid[i].length === rowsPerReel[i].
