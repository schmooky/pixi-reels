Most of these demos are built on a pin. You pin a cell once and the engine draws the pinned symbol over the spinning reel, counts down its turns and removes it when they run out. The wilds come first, then multipliers, held wins and mystery reveals.

## Wilds

<a id="sticky-wild-pin"></a>
<a id="sticky-wild-cellpin"></a>

### Sticky wild

The smallest pin. When a wild lands, pin its cell with `turns: N`. The engine keeps it on screen while the reel spins underneath and counts the turns down, so there are no extra sprites to manage and nothing to write into the next result grid.

### Expanding wild

A wild lands and the whole reel turns wild for N spins. The pins expire on their own when the turn counter reaches zero.

### Walking wild

A wild moves one reel left on each spin with `reelSet.movePin()`. The flight hooks are where a Spine symbol switches to its run animation.

Move pins one at a time with `await`, not with `Promise.all`. Two pins moving to the same cell throws.

### Animated swap

`setSymbolAt` swaps a symbol at once. `swapSymbols` plays the old symbols out, swaps them, holds, and plays the new ones in, with a stagger on each side.

```ts
await reelSet.swapSymbols(
  [0, 1, 2].map((cell) => ({ reel: 1, cell, id: 'WILD' })),
  { outDelay: (_, i) => i * 0.06, holdMs: 200, inDelay: (_, i) => i * 0.09 },
);
```

It also handles the details a hand-written reveal tends to miss. It raises the zIndex so an entrance that overshoots its cell is not clipped by the next cell, and it hides the new symbol after the swap, because re-activation resets `alpha` to 1 and the new art would show for a frame before its entrance.

Each step can be left out. Pass `skipOut` when the art plays its own Spine `out` track, `skipIn` to leave the cells hidden and reveal them yourself, and `onSwapped` for a step in the middle that has to wait for an animation. `playIn()` and `playOut()` are the per-symbol hooks underneath, on the same contract as `playDestroy`. Override them for entrances and exits that suit your art.

Cells are checked before anything starts, and an abort still performs the swap, so the board always matches the result. It works only at rest and only on single-cell symbols. A big symbol covers cells the frame layer reserves, so revealing one is a `setResult` or `setShape` job.

## Other reel directions

### Sticky wilds on reels that spin up

A `.direction('reverse')` set. The reels travel upward through three respins while the pinned wilds hold their cells, and new wilds that land on the way stick too.

Pin coordinates are geometric. Cell 0 is the top cell whichever edge the strip feeds from.
