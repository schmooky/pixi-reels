New in v2: travel is a property of the orientation axis, not the sign of a delta. One engine spins vertical or horizontal, forward or reverse, per reel - and the standalone HorizontalReel is retired.

<a id="horizontal-banner"></a>

## Horizontal banner

A one-reel sideways strip - the "paying symbols this round" banner. Just `.orientation('horizontal')` on the normal builder; same spin lifecycle, events, and speed profiles as a vertical set.

<a id="banner-ways"></a>

## Banner that joins a ways win

A horizontal banner above a 5x3 ways set. The banner is a sixth reel: a wild in the cell above a column makes that column count, extending the way. Two ReelSets, one presentation (the ReelStage composition); the engine spins and lands both, the game scripts the ways math and spotlights the cells.

**Gotcha:** pixi-reels never computes wins - the recipe owns the ways evaluation and spotlight; the library only spins and lands.

## Roll-up (reverse direction)

Every reel spins upward: one `.direction('reverse')` call. The same engine, events and landing bounce just run the other way.

## Mixed direction per reel

`.directionPerReel([...])` gives each column its own travel direction for a woven, alternating look. Every reel still lands the exact server frame.
