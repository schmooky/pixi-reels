<a id="hold-and-win-horizontal-lock"></a>

## Hold & Win: cells that spin sideways

The whole lock-and-respin round on `.orientation('horizontal')`, built straight on a ReelSet with CellPin: a pin claims a cell, the strip cannot overwrite it, and the engine holds the coin on screen while the row scrolls past behind it.

**Gotcha:** `HoldAndWinBuilder` and `BoardGrid` build every cell as a vertical 1x1 ReelSet and expose no axis knob, so a board on another axis is a ReelSet plus pins.

<RecipeDemo code="hold-and-win-horizontal-lock" height={340} />

<a id="hold-and-win-reverse-respin"></a>

## Hold & Win: free cells roll upward

The same lock on a `.direction('reverse')` set, plus the spin option that pairs with it: a reel whose every cell is already locked goes into `spin({ holdReels })` and sits the wave out entirely.

**Gotcha:** `setResult` still wants a full reel-count grid. entries at held indices are ignored, not omitted.
