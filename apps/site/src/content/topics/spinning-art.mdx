A symbol at rest can be anything: a Spine skeleton, an animated sprite, a
container of layers. Register it through `StaticSpinSymbol` and give it a
`createInner` that builds the real symbol. At rest the inner symbol is live,
so idle loops and win animations play as usual. When the reel starts, the
wrapper deactivates it and spins a plain sprite of it instead, and on the
landing it brings the inner symbol back on the final id.

The sprite's texture comes from a `SpinTextureCache` shared by the set, which
snapshots each id once. With `spinTexture: 'blurred'` it also bakes a motion
blur along the reel's travel and crossfades to it over `blurRampMs`. No
filter runs during the spin, and no skeleton ticks while the strip moves.
`prewarmSpinTextures` bakes every id before the first spin; otherwise each
one is baked the first time it is needed. Where you have hand-drawn blur
strips, `cache.setBlurred(id, texture)` makes the cache use them instead.
