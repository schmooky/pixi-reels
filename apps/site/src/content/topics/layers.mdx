Every symbol lives inside its reel, and every reel sits under the viewport
mask. That keeps the board tidy until a symbol's art is bigger than its cell:
a scatter with a glow, a wild with a crown, a winning symbol that should rise
above the rest.

There are three ways out, from smallest to largest. A `zIndex` in the
symbol's data draws it above the other symbols in the same reel. Unmasking
moves it to the layer above every reel and above the mask, so it can spill
past the frame. Promoting it through the spotlight lifts one instance for as
long as a win shows, then puts it back.

The board primitive at the end of the page is the same cell layout without
the reels: a grid you fill and animate yourself.
